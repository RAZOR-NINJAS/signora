# Signora Complete Sign & Gesture Catalog

This document describes all signs supported by Signora across ASL Fingerspelling, Indian Sign Language (ISL), and ASL Dynamic Words.

---

## 1. ASL Fingerspelling (Single-Handed)

All 26 English letters can be formed with either your dominant hand or non-dominant hand (Signora automatically normalizes handedness).

| Sign | Description & Hand Formation Tips |
| :---: | :--- |
| **A** | Make a fist with thumb straight up resting flat against the outer side of the index finger. |
| **B** | Hold 4 fingers upright touching together; fold thumb flat across your palm. |
| **C** | Curve fingers and thumb into a gentle 'C' arc. |
| **D** | Index finger points straight up; thumb touches tips of middle, ring, and pinky. |
| **E** | Curl all 4 fingers down tightly onto palm; tuck thumb flat beneath them. |
| **F** | Touch index and thumb tips together into a small circle; extend other 3 fingers up. |
| **G** | Point index finger and thumb horizontally forward in parallel (side-on pinch). |
| **H** | Index and middle fingers extended together horizontally forward; thumb tucked below. |
| **I** | Fist with pinky finger pointing straight up. |
| **J** | Form 'I' handshape, then trace letter 'J' curve in the air. |
| **K** | Index finger upright, middle finger angled forward, thumb tucked between them. |
| **L** | Extend thumb and index at 90° to form an 'L'; curl other fingers into palm. |
| **M** | Thumb tucked beneath 3 fingers (index, middle, ring); pinky curled on the side. |
| **N** | Thumb tucked beneath 2 fingers (index, middle); other fingers curled into palm. |
| **O** | Curve all fingertip pads to meet thumb pad, forming an 'O' ring. |
| **P** | Same finger formation as 'K', but oriented downwards toward the ground. |
| **Q** | Same finger formation as 'G', but oriented downwards toward the ground. |
| **R** | Cross middle finger over index finger; curl remaining fingers. |
| **S** | Tight fist with thumb crossed horizontally over the front of the curled fingers. |
| **T** | Fist with thumb tucked between index and middle fingers. |
| **U** | Index and middle fingers held together straight up; thumb crosses ring finger. |
| **V** | Index and middle fingers spread apart upright in a 'V' peace sign. |
| **W** | Index, middle, and ring fingers spread upright in a 'W'; thumb holds pinky down. |
| **X** | Fist with index finger crooked like a curved hook or claw. |
| **Y** | Extend thumb and pinky outward (shaka sign); curl middle three fingers. |
| **Z** | Extend index finger and trace 'Z' zigzag horizontally in the air. |
| **SPACE** | Hold hand relaxed with thumb resting against the palm center for 800 ms. |

## 2. Indian Sign Language (ISL Two-Handed Alphabet & Static Words)

ISL standard dual-hand manual alphabet and iconic signs according to ISLRTC specifications.

### Manual Alphabet (Two-Handed)
- **A:** Dominant hand index touches the thumb tip of the base hand.
- **B:** Both open hands with fingers touching to form two loops.
- **C:** Dominant hand forms a 'C' shape over the base hand palm.
- **D:** Dominant index forms the upright stem against base hand loop.
- **E:** Dominant index touches the index fingertip of the base hand.
- **F:** Index and middle fingers of dominant hand cross base hand fingers.
- **G:** Both fists stacked vertically with dominant fist on top.
- **H:** Dominant open flat hand sweeps across base open flat hand.
- **I:** Dominant index touches the middle fingertip of the base hand.
- **K:** Dominant index hooks over base index finger.
- **L:** Dominant 'L' hand resting on base flat palm.
- **M:** Dominant 3 fingers placed on base open palm.
- **N:** Dominant 2 fingers placed on base open palm.
- **O:** Dominant index touches ring fingertip of the base hand.
- **P:** Dominant index and thumb form loop around base index finger.
- **Q:** Dominant thumb and index loop hooked around base wrist.
- **R:** Dominant index rests on base open palm.
- **S:** Dominant pinky hooks base pinky.
- **T:** Dominant index touches edge of base index.
- **U:** Dominant index touches base pinky fingertip.
- **V:** Dominant 'V' fingers resting on base open palm.
- **W:** Both hands interlocking fingers upright.
- **X:** Index fingers of both hands crossed into an 'X'.
- **Y:** Dominant index placed between thumb and index of base hand.

### Static ISL Words
- **NAMASTE:** Both open flat hands joined together palm-to-palm at chest level in prayer greeting.
- **GOOD:** Thumbs up gesture.
- **BAD:** Pinky finger extended upright.
- **PEACE:** Index and middle fingers upright in 'V' formation.
- **OK:** Thumb and index tips joined in a circle, other three fingers upright.
- **HOUSE:** Fingertips of both angled flat hands touching to form a pitched roof.
- **BOOK:** Both flat hands held together side-by-side touching pinky edges like an open book.
- **TIME:** Dominant index finger pointing directly at the base wrist watch location.
- **LOVE:** Thumb, index, and pinky extended upright (I-L-Y sign).
- **STOP:** Open flat palm facing straight forward toward camera.

## 3. ASL Dynamic Words (Holistic Motion DTW-kNN)

Dynamic words evaluated over 30-frame temporal trajectories:

1. **HELLO:** Open flat hand moving outward from temple in a salute wave.
2. **THANKYOU:** Flat hand moving outward from chin/lips toward camera.
3. **PLEASE:** Flat open hand rubbing circular motion over chest.
4. **YES:** Fist nodding up and down like a head nod.
5. **NO:** Index and middle fingers snapping down against thumb like a beak.
6. **HELP:** Fist resting on top of flat open base hand, lifting upward together.
7. **SORRY:** Fist rubbing in circular motion over chest.
8. **GOOD:** Flat hand moving from lips down onto base flat hand.
9. **BAD:** Flat hand moving from chin turning palm downward.
10. **NAME:** 'H' fingers of dominant hand tapping twice across 'H' fingers of base hand.
11. **MORE:** Both hands with fingertips clustered tapping together twice in front of chest.
12. **LOVE:** Both arms crossed across chest in a hug gesture.
13. **EAT:** Clustered fingers tapping near lips.
14. **DRINK:** 'C' hand brought to lips mimicking drinking from a glass.
15. **WHERE:** Index finger wagging back and forth side to side.
16. **FINISHED:** Both open hands facing in, then flicking outward with palms facing down.
